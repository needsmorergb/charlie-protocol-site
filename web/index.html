The Sol-Incinerator Deflationary Flywheel

The core mission: combat Solana network inflation by turning trade volume into permanent SOL destruction. Trading creator fees are not hoarded—they are funneled into the Sol-Incinerator where lamports are permanently purged from the global ledger.

  1. STEP 01

    Trading Accrues Creator Fees

    Every trade on the bonding curve or AMM accrues fees on-chain automatically from market volume.

  2. STEP 02

    Fees Route to Sol-Incinerator

    Creator fees are funneled straight into 1nc1nerator..., Solana’s native burning address.

  3. STEP 03

    Purged at Block Boundary

    The Solana runtime permanently deletes all lamports credited to the Incinerator at each block boundary.

  4. STEP 04

    Decreasing Network Inflation

    Global SOL supply contracts permanently. Verifiable deflation attracts volume that funds the next burn.
