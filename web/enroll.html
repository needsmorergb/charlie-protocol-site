Configure Fee Split

pump.fun routes a share of every trade to the coin’s creator vault. This split decides where that share goes, and it is committed permanently on-chain.

      How enrollment works on chain
      

        

          
Single Atomic Transaction

          
If your coin has no fee-sharing config yet, the transaction bundles create_fee_sharing_config and update_fee_shares_v2 in one atomic execution. If it already has one, it executes update_fee_shares_v2.

        

        

          
Permanent Revocation

          
The transaction passes admin_revoked to pump's fee sharing program. Once confirmed, the config is frozen irrevocably and cannot be altered by anyone.

        

        

          
Decentralized Crank Payouts

          
Creator fees accumulate in pump's native vault. The protocol's hourly keeper cranks trigger distributions permissionlessly—no ongoing signatures required.

        

        

          
Continuous SOL Deflation

          
Every payout to 1nc1nerator11111111111111111111111111111111 destroys lamports at the block boundary, directly counteracting Solana network inflation.
